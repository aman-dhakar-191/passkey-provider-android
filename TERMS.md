## 2. What the app does

websites and apps, on the phone and, via QR code, on a computer. It needs Android 14 or newer with a
screen lock. How it handles information is described in the
[privacy policy](https://aman-dhakar-191.github.io/passkey-provider-android/privacy/).

## 5. Your passkeys can be lost
Passkeys are stored only on your phone and are **not backed up or synced**, on purpose, so they can't

the app, clear its storage, or remove your screen lock. We can't recover them, because we never have a
copy. Deleting a passkey in the app doesn't remove it from the website's account settings.
