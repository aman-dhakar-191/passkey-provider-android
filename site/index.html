What it does

Works where you sign in

When you sign in to an app or website, your phone offers Passkey Vault. Just tap Continue.

One touch

Your fingerprint or screen lock approves each sign-in. Nothing to type.

Your computer too

On a computer choose "Use a phone or tablet" and scan the QR code with your phone.

Stays on your phone

Keys are made in the phone's secure hardware and never leave it. No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads.

Stops phishing

A passkey only works on the website it was made for, and only trusted browsers and verified apps can use it.

Get started

  1. Install Passkey Vault (Android 14 or newer, with a screen lock).
  2. Open the app and tap Passkey settings. Turn Passkey Vault on and choose it as the preferred service.
  3. Create a passkey on any site that offers one. Next time, sign in with your fingerprint.

Passkeys are stored only on this phone and aren't synced or backed up. Keep another way to sign in to important accounts.
