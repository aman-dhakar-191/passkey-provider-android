Sign in with your fingerprint, not a password.

Passkey Vault keeps your passkeys on your Android phone. Use them in apps, in your browser, and on your computer by scanning a QR code.

Download for Android See how it works

Free. Needs Android 14 or newer and a screen lock. Coming soon to Indus Appstore.

How it works

Set it up once. After that, signing in is a tap and a touch.

  1. Turn it on

    Install the app, tap Passkey settings and choose Passkey Vault as your passkey service.

  2. Choose Passkey Vault

    When a website or app asks for a passkey, your phone offers Passkey Vault. Tap Continue.

  3. Touch the sensor

    Your fingerprint or screen lock confirms it's you, and you're signed in.

Your computer too

Your phone signs you in to websites on your laptop or desktop. Nothing to install on the computer.

  1. On the computer, choose Use a phone or tablet when the site asks for a passkey.
  2. Open Passkey Vault and tap Scan QR code.
  3. Touch the fingerprint sensor. The computer is signed in.

Uses Bluetooth to check your phone is near the computer.

Your keys stay with you

Each passkey is locked inside your phone and can't be copied out, not even by us.

What we get

Nothing.

No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads. A passkey only works on the real website it was made for, so fake sites can't trick you. Read the privacy policy.

Keep a second way in

Passkeys aren't backed up, so if you lose your phone, you lose them. Keep another way to sign in to important accounts, like a password, recovery codes or a passkey on another device. The app's New or lost phone? guide walks you through it.

Get Passkey Vault

Free and open source. Needs Android 14 or newer and a screen lock.

  1. Download and install the app.
  2. Open it and tap Passkey settings. Turn Passkey Vault on and choose it as your preferred service.
  3. Next time a website offers passkeys, create one. After that, sign in with your fingerprint.
